## The Real Cost of Manual Processes

Let's do some simple math:

### Scenario: A Small Consulting Firm

**Manual bookkeeping:**
- 10 hours per week on data entry and reconciliation
- Employee cost: $30/hour
- Annual cost: 10 hours × 52 weeks × $30 = **$15,600**

**Automated bookkeeping:**
- Initial setup: $2,000
- Monthly software cost: $100
- Annual cost: $2,000 + ($100 × 12) = **$3,200**

**Annual savings: $12,400**

And that's just one process.

## The Hidden Costs You're Not Counting

Most businesses only calculate direct labor costs. But automation saves money in ways you might not realize:

### 1. Error Reduction
- Manual data entry has a 1-4% error rate
- Each error costs time to find and fix
- Some errors result in compliance penalties

### 2. Opportunity Cost
- Time spent on manual tasks is time NOT spent on revenue-generating activities
- If your $100/hour consultant spends 10 hours/week on admin, that's $52,000/year in lost billable time

### 3. Employee Satisfaction
- Employees hate repetitive tasks
- Automation reduces burnout and turnover
- Replacing an employee costs 50-200% of their annual salary

### 4. Scalability
- Manual processes don't scale
- Automation handles 10x the volume without additional cost

## How to Calculate Your ROI

### Step 1: Identify the Process
What manual task takes the most time?

### Step 2: Calculate Current Cost
- Hours per week × hourly rate × 52 weeks
- Add error costs and opportunity costs

### Step 3: Estimate Automation Cost
- One-time setup fee
- Monthly software costs
- Training time

### Step 4: Calculate Payback Period
- Total automation cost ÷ monthly savings = months to break even

### Step 5: Calculate 3-Year ROI
- (3-year savings - automation cost) ÷ automation cost × 100
