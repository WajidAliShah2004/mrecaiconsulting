# 5 AI Tools Every Small Business Needs in 2026 (That Aren't ChatGPT)

Everyone knows about ChatGPT. But while it's great for writing emails and brainstorming, it's not the only AI tool your business needs.

If you're new to business automation, start with our [Executive's Guide to AI: How to Automate Your Business Without Increasing Risk](/resources/executives-guide-to-ai) for a complete framework on implementing AI safely and effectively.

Here are five specialized AI tools that deliver measurable ROI for small businesses:

## 1. Zapier/Make - The Automation Connector

**What it does**: Connects your different software tools and automates workflows between them.

**Use case**: When a new lead fills out your website form, automatically add them to your CRM, send a welcome email, and create a task for follow-up.

**ROI**: Saves 5-10 hours per week on manual data entry.

## 2. Otter.ai - Meeting Intelligence

**What it does**: Transcribes meetings in real-time and extracts action items automatically.

**Use case**: Never take notes during client calls again. Get a full transcript and summary delivered to your inbox minutes after the meeting ends.

**ROI**: Improves meeting productivity by 30% and ensures nothing falls through the cracks.

## 3. QuickBooks + Receipt Bank - Financial Automation

**What it does**: Uses OCR to scan receipts and invoices, automatically categorizing them in your accounting software.

**Use case**: Snap a photo of a receipt with your phone. The AI reads it, categorizes it, and logs it in QuickBooks automatically.

**ROI**: Reduces bookkeeping time by 60-70%. [Learn more about our Tax & Accounting services](/services/tax-services).

## 4. HubSpot AI - CRM Intelligence

**What it does**: Predicts which leads are most likely to convert and suggests the best time to follow up.

**Use case**: Your CRM tells you "Contact John Smith today - he's 85% likely to buy based on his engagement patterns."

**ROI**: Increases conversion rates by 20-30% through better lead prioritization.

## 5. Grammarly Business - Professional Communication

**What it does**: AI-powered writing assistant that ensures all your business communications are professional and error-free.

**Use case**: Automatically checks emails, proposals, and documents for tone, clarity, and grammar before they go out.

**ROI**: Prevents embarrassing mistakes and improves professional image.
